### Preventing Valuable News from Being Lost

Do not treat the initial shortlist as the complete set of stories eligible for the final episode.

Your first selection pass identifies the strongest headlines, not every story worth covering.

After ranking the news, perform a separate **missed-story recovery pass** across all remaining candidates.

Look specifically for:

* Important stories that narrowly missed the ranking cutoff.
* Useful product launches, model updates, and newly available capabilities.
* Programs, credits, free access, pricing changes, and opportunities audiences can act on.
* Policy, legal, and access developments that could affect users or developers.
* Significant company developments, funding, partnerships, and competitive moves.
* Smaller stories that provide information not already covered by a selected story.

Do not assume that a story is unimportant merely because another story scored higher. Ranking determines order and relative importance; it does not automatically determine eligibility for coverage.

For each excluded story, explicitly decide whether it should be a full segment, a quick brief, merged into another story, or excluded.

Before excluding a credible story with meaningful audience value, ask:

1. Does it provide new information that the selected stories do not cover?
2. Would a user, developer, researcher, business, or AI enthusiast reasonably want to know about it today?
3. Does it offer something to try, access, purchase, claim, understand, or monitor?
4. Does it have material business, legal, financial, technical, or industry implications?
5. Could it be covered briefly without sacrificing more important news?

If the story provides distinct value, retain it for consideration in the full episode or quick-catch-up section.

Exclude stories only when there is a specific reason, such as duplication, insufficient credibility, lack of meaningful new information, negligible audience value, or irrelevance.

**The objective is not to maximize the number of headlines. It is to minimize the number of genuinely worthwhile developments that the audience misses.**
